About Me

I'm a final-year B.Sc. Software Engineering student at Daffodil International University in Dhaka (CGPA 3.87/4.00, graduating July 2027). My focus is machine learning, AI and research: I like taking a question, designing an experiment around it, and letting the results decide.

Right now I'm running independent research on small language models and on-device AI agents, with two papers in progress on quantization, failure prediction and tool use in models under 3B parameters. Before that, I built ML projects across regression, computer vision and transfer learning, and I helped build the frontend of TryOn.ai, a live generative-AI product.

Outside research, I'm an Executive Member of the DIU Data Science Club, where I run peer workshops on data science and AI tools, and of the DIU Model United Nations Club. I also teach: I tutor young students and make computer science explainer videos on YouTube.

Research

INDEPENDENT RESEARCH · 2026 – PRESENT

Small Language Models & On-Device AI Agents

How reliable are small (sub-3B) language models when they run as agents on ordinary CPUs, and can we tell in advance when they'll fail? I design the experiments, prepare the evaluation data and analyse the results in Python.

  • Paper 1 · in progress “Within-Task or Between-Task? Quantization and the Limits of Harness-Visible Failure Prediction in Sub-3B Agent Loops on CPU.”
  • Paper 2 · in progress “Can Small Models Use a Premortem They Can’t Write?”

UNDERGRADUATE THESIS · DIU

Multi-Label Emotion Classification in Bangla Text

Extending single-label Bangla emotion datasets into multi-label ones and benchmarking machine learning approaches on them. Bangla is widely spoken but under-resourced in NLP, and this work aims to close part of that gap.
